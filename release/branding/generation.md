Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, not the API/CLI fallback. The original raster is preserved in `logo-generated.png`; the packaged app icon is in the app asset catalog.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished, exceptionally clean iOS App Store app icon for an independent Minsk parking app named Stajanka / Стаянка. One square 1024x1024 image, full bleed, opaque background. Existing app palette: deep forest green #17332B and soft vibrant lime #D0E888. Main symbol: a distinctive geometric parking P monogram that subtly integrates the downward point of a location marker, using a single confident thick lime shape and crisp negative space, centered and optically balanced. Sophisticated restrained European civic mobility design, recognizable at 48px, flat vector-like precision, gently rounded internal geometry. Dark forest green fills the entire square; symbol occupies about 65 percent. No wordmark, no letters besides the symbolic P, no cars, no road scene, no license plates, no mockup, no phone frame, no outer rounded-square mask, no gradient, no texture, no glow, no drop shadow, no decorative border. Original brand mark; do not mimic Parkouka's logo or any third-party icon.
