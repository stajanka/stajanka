Stajanka is an independent native parking client for Minsk, Belarus. Stajanka has no app-account registration or sign-in and no Parkouka account-authentication screen. The previous optional Parkouka account screen, remote account history and remote vehicle registration have been removed. No demo login or credentials are needed for review. The app does not request a Parkouka email or password and removes its old saved account-session cookies on upgrade.

Map browsing, the garage, price calculation, payment checkout and paid-time verification all work without an app account. Vehicles and parking records are stored in an on-device SQLite database, with migration of earlier local records. History contains parking recorded or confirmed on this device, without cloud synchronization or remote account-history retrieval. Public checks by plate may restore current paid coverage, but do not import past transactions or unknown amounts.

Review the map remotely without granting location: it opens in central Minsk. Search for a street, select a parking zone, add a demonstration plate and select a duration to inspect the calculation. Do not complete a real parking payment unless you intend to purchase that real-world service. ERIP and bePaid payments pay the parking operator, not for digital app features; the app adds no subscription or service fee. Banks and payment providers may require authentication within their own checkout interface.

The default interface is Belarusian. Use the globe in the garage to switch to Russian or English. Tap a plate at checkout to switch/add a vehicle. A visible garage Delete button removes a vehicle locally and retains its parking records. About and data contains bundled policies, support and full local-data deletion, which removes the garage, local history, reminders and payment web data held by Stajanka. No vehicle ownership documents or fines are requested.

Card checkout keeps bank/3-D Secure pages visible, processes the merchant return without displaying the merchant website, then independently verifies the paid period. A redirect alone never marks a payment successful. Verification runs while the app is foregrounded; local notifications require permission. The selected plate, zone and parking parameters still go to Parkouka over HTTPS; external payment pages retain their payment, security and analytics processing as disclosed in the privacy policy.

Screenshots use fictional demonstration vehicles. No personal account or real user's plate is shown.

Stajanka is an independent client and does not claim affiliation with Parkouka.by or the parking operator.
